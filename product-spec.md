# ArtMneme - Product Specification (v1)

## Overview

ArtMneme is a personal artwork collection and exploration application.

The application helps users:

- collect artworks they have personally encountered
- remember artworks over time
- record details and observations
- explore relationships between artworks
- maintain a watchlist of artworks they have not yet encountered

The application is centered around personal collections, with optional community discovery features.

---

# Problem Statement

People often encounter artworks in museums, galleries, books, social media, or while traveling.

Existing solutions such as:

- phone photo galleries
- note-taking apps
- spreadsheets
- bookmarks

allow storage but provide little support for exploration and rediscovery.
